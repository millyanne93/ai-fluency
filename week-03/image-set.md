## Rejection Note

I generated three hero image options using AI. One was a generic "server room" stock image; it felt disconnected from my work and looked like something from a 2010 tech blog. Another was a geometric abstract that looked nice but said nothing about backend engineering. I rejected both.

Instead of a generated hero image, I'll use a clean section title over whitespace. My real screenshots (Swagger UI, Planit dashboard, Trackr dashboard) will be the visual centrepiece; they prove my work, and they're the loudest thing on the page.

## Why I Chose Real Captures

Every image in my portfolio serves a purpose: proving my work. I chose:
- Swagger UI screenshots because they show my API design
- Architecture diagrams because they show my infrastructure decisions
- Dashboard screenshots because they show my full-stack work

No stock photos. No decorative graphics. Just evidence of what I've built.
