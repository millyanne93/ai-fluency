### Stack Rationale
**Chosen stack:** Plain HTML/CSS with AI, hosted on Netlify

**Why I chose this:** It's free, simple, and I can maintain it myself. I can explain every part of it.

**Alternatives considered:**
- No-code (Framer): Fast, but I'd be locked into their platform.
- Framework (Next.js): Overkill for a portfolio — I'd spend more time fixing builds than showing my work.

**Can I maintain this?** Yes — plain HTML is simple, I wrote it myself, and I understand every line.
